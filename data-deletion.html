Data Deletion Instructions

mirstock (app: Mirkun AutoPost) only keeps commenter IDs/names, comment text and records of replies/messages sent.

  1. Send a DM to @mirstock.kr on Instagram, or leave a note on mirstock.tistory.com, saying "data deletion request" with your account name.
  2. We will delete all records related to your account within 30 days and confirm through the same channel.
  3. Your comments on each platform can be deleted directly on that platform.

You can also remove Mirkun AutoPost under "Apps and Websites" in your Facebook/Instagram settings.
